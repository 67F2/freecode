$ man free-agents

The software: genuinely free — most of these are open source. The models: usually the catch. Most agents want YOUR API key, which means heavy usage = an invoice from your model provider with your name on it. Real exceptions: Antigravity (Google covers you), Copilot Free (capped, but covered), and OpenCode Zen (free hosted models).

Cloud tools send your code to model providers — that's the deal. Paranoid? Point a terminal agent at a local model: Pi (minimal, MIT, built for exactly this — ollama launch pi and you're done) or Cline in VS Code. Your code never leaves your machine. Your secrets stay yours, including the hardcoded API key in config.py we won't mention.

Only ~29% of developers fully trust AI output — which is still more than trust project estimates. These tools are fast typists, not architects. They excel at boilerplate and pattern-matching; they struggle with security edge cases and the business logic living only in Dave's head. Keep your tests green and expectations calibrated.

The lazy decision tree: live in the terminal? → Antigravity CLI (actually free) or Aider (git-native). Live in VS Code? → Cline (agentic), Copilot Free (easiest), or Antigravity's extension. Want zero cloud contact ever? → Pi + Ollama. Zero setup AND zero keys, and a text ad won't hurt you? → Freebuff. Still stuck? Take the quiz above, it's medically inadvisable but fun.

Yes — point aider's key slot at a free API tier and you're genuinely at $0: Google AI Studio (Gemini Flash at ~1,500 req/day; Pro models fled to the paid tier in April 2026 and aren't returning our calls), OpenRouter :free models (28+ including Qwen3 Coder 480B — strongest free coder alive — but only 50 req/day until you slip OpenRouter a one-time $10, the best bribe in tech, which bumps you to 1,000/day forever), Groq (~1,000 req/day of Llama moving fast enough to warm your room), Cerebras (~1M tokens/day of whatever open-weight model is trendy this week) and Mistral (~1B tokens/month, provided you consent to being training data — the free cheese is watching your prompt). The fine print: free ≠ frontier — nobody's handing out Claude Opus via API, quotas evaporate exactly when you hit flow state, free endpoints vanish overnight like a bad tenant, and some providers read your prompts like a diary. Great for side projects; bring a paid key for anything with a deadline.

Constantly. Free tiers mutate weekly in the AI arms race. This page reflects reality as of August 2026. Verify before making financial decisions — or emotional ones, like falling in love with a pricing plan.
